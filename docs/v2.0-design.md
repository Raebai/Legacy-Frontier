# Legacy Frontier v2.0 — Design (the co-op roguelite tower-climber pivot)

- **Date:** 2026-07-08
- **Status:** Design locked in brainstorm; spec under review before planning.
- **Supersedes as active direction:** `v1.0-design.md` (3D action-RPG, branch `v1.0-3d`) — preserved but parked.
- **Reuses:** the entire 2D + AI-memory stack shipped on `main` (v0.0–v0.5).

---

## 1. Why this pivot

The v1.0 3D open-world action-RPG (17 regions, Empyrean Spire, combat + loot + co-op) is a studio-years project, not a solo-evenings one. The pivot driver is **solo-feasibility**, not loss of interest. This design keeps everything the maker loves while cutting the scope that was quietly turning the dream into a graveyard.

Crucially, most of this pivot **reduces** scope: 2D instead of 3D (the biggest solo-dev art win), floor/room runs instead of an open world (~90% less content surface), mobile-first (always the locked input model, D-011). The one axis it *raises* — multiplayer — is deliberately staged to the end (§11).

---

## 2. North star

> **A mobile-first 2D top-down co-op roguelite spell-brawler about climbing an endless Tower** — fast, hard floor-by-floor runs that feed a **living hub of AI-memory NPCs** who witness your ascent and remember your legend.

**Soul Knight's form factor. Hades' soul. Tower of God's structure. A Cuphead-tier skill ceiling. And a world that is actually alive — the thing no competitor can retrofit.**

The soul of the game is **social**. Single-player-first is scaffolding to reach that soul safely (§11), not the destination.

---

## 3. Inspirations (and what each contributes)

- **Soul Knight** — the proven mobile top-down co-op roguelite spell-brawler form factor; auto-aim touch controls.
- **Wizards of Legend** — loadout/arcana casting, dash-based movement, deep build variety.
- **Magicka 2** — chaotic co-op spellcasting energy (the *feel*, not the input model).
- **Terraria** — progression via loot + movement unlocks; optional brutal bosses; "my own journey."
- **Cuphead / modded Terraria bosses** — hard, learnable, pattern-mastery boss fights.
- **Mini Militia** — dead-simple pick-up-and-play local multiplayer; social virality.
- **Hades** — a hub of characters you build relationships with between runs; deep-but-optional story; the roguelite-with-soul template.
- **Tower of God** — the **unifying structure** (§5): floors, tests, ascension = power + status, **Rankers** who descend to help, deep lore, names/rank as identity.

---

## 4. The pillars (the moats)

We are entering Soul Knight's space. We **cannot** out-content or out-polish an established studio. The only way to matter is a moat they structurally cannot or will not copy. All four are committed as core identity:

1. **Living AI hub.** The landing's NPCs *remember your climbs* and react — hand you a fire-ward because a floor guardian killed you three times, track your legend as you ascend. A 2017-engine studio game can't retrofit this; a big studio won't risk LLM unpredictability in a polished product. A solo dev in 2026 with local LLMs and a four-layer memory system *already written* can. **This is the moat.** *(reuses existing tech)*

2. **Co-op AI social reactions — the viral wedge.** In co-op the NPCs remember your whole *party*, play favorites, and react to the group after a climb (roast whoever fled, celebrate whoever carried). Nobody shares "I cleared a floor"; everybody shares "the AI shopkeeper called my friend a coward by name." The "pop off" engine; serves the Mini-Militia / everyone-plays thesis. *(lands with co-op; a north star from day one)*

3. **Semantic aura / legend.** Your reputation is *understood and narrated* by the world — "the reckless fire-mage who never retreats" is a thing NPCs know and treat you by. In Tower terms: your *name as a climber*. Deep personalization, longevity, "my own story." The connective tissue between combat and hub.

4. **Cuphead-tier floor guardians.** Brutal-but-fair, learnable, pattern-rich bosses as the test at each floor's top. Mobile roguelites are mostly easy/grindy; this is the positioning wedge — *"the mobile roguelite for people who want to get good."* Loops with the hub, which witnesses your deaths and triumphs.

**The throughline:** Raebai, the project's first NPC (v0.0), was designed as *"the chronicler — a witness, not a judge."* Here that role snaps into place: he *records your ascent.* The oldest idea in the project is the keystone of the new one.

---

## 5. The Tower — unifying structure

The Tower is the meta-structure that connects every pillar. It is a **framing**, not a pile of new systems — it costs nothing extra in the slice.

- **A landing = the hub.** Between climbs you rest on a landing (Raebai + other living NPCs).
- **A climb = a run.** Ascending to the next floor is a roguelite run of procedural rooms.
- **A floor guardian = a boss.** Each floor's test is a hard, learnable boss (pillar 4).
- **Ascension = meta-progression + identity.** How high you've climbed is your rank, your power, your *name* (pillar 3).
- **Rankers = OP helpers.** Players who've climbed high become powerful, storied Rankers who can **descend to help newcomers** — the grinder's reward is being a hero to others (§11, rung 3). The living world *narrates* famous Rankers (fuses pillars 2 + 3).
- **Shared landings = public social zones.** The MMO-feel summit (§11, rung 4).
- **The Tower's mystery = the optional lore** (§6).

**Continuity:** the parked 3D design was built around the *Empyrean Spire* — a 250-unit tower connecting three vertical layers. The tower fixation was always present; this genre is where it becomes the entire game.

### 5.1 Progression, Rank & Clan Culture (the long arc, post-Slice-1)

A coherent progression/social culture synthesized from **Tower of God** + genre ranking systems (Souls covenants & summon culture, Warframe Mastery Rank / syndicates / clans + dojos, Destiny raid prestige, cultivation-novel ascension ladders where the higher you go the fewer and mightier you are). This is the **long-arc vision** the aura/legend + social pillars grow into. **None of it is in Slice 0/1** (which has one class, one guardian, one or two boons). It lives here so early systems don't contradict it.

*How Tower of God actually structures its world, and how we borrow it:*
- **Regulars → Rankers → High Rankers.** In ToG, climbers are *Regulars*; those who climb high enough become immensely powerful, numbered *Rankers* (rank = literal status/celebrity). We map this to **Climbers → Ranked → Rankers.** Everyone starts a nameless Climber; ascending milestone floors earns **Rank** — a diegetic tier + title that *is* your identity and status. The highest become **Rankers** who can **descend to aid** lower Climbers (the summoning/helper reward — MP ladder rung 3). The grind's payoff is power *and* becoming a legend others encounter.
- **Positions → Roles.** ToG teams run functional positions — *Fisherman* (frontline melee), *Spear Bearer* (ranged), *Wave Controller* (energy/AoE), *Light Bearer* (support/navigation), *Scout*, *Guide*. We borrow these as **co-op roles** that create team-comp synergy — mapping onto or complementing classes.
- **Shinsu → Essence.** ToG's fundamental power is *Shinsu* ("god water"); mastery = strength. We use **Essence**, a diegetic energy you command more of as you ascend — ties power scaling to the Tower fiction rather than raw stat inflation.
- **Administrators → Floor Guardians.** ToG floors are ruled by rule-enforcing *Administrators* who set the tests. Our **Guardians** are the hard, learnable bosses (pillar 4) whose defeat yields **signature powers/weapons** — build-defining, aura-defining relics tied to that floor. Climbing unlocks *new ways to play*, not just bigger numbers. (This is the "reward for going higher" the maker asked for.)
- **The Families / FUG / Wolhaiksong → Clans.** ToG's factions are clans with histories, philosophies, and rivalries. Our **Clans** are player factions with shared banners/auras, collective goals, and rivalries — the social glue + retention + culture layer, and the heart of the MMO-feel landings.
- **Titles & Aura.** In ToG your name and title (e.g. "Slayer," a Princess of Jahad) precede you. Rank + deeds + clan + role = your **narrated identity** (the aura pillar). NPCs — and eventually other players — read and speak your title.

**One culture, one sentence:** *The Tower is a society of Climbers; you rise from nameless to Ranked to Ranker, and your floor-won powers, your role, your clan, and your title are your aura — witnessed and narrated by a living world.*

**Sequencing:** a rank *number/title* is cheap and can seed early (even Slice-adjacent). Unique floor powers arrive with more floors/guardians. Roles arrive with co-op. Clans arrive in the social era (MP ladder rungs 3–4). All post-Slice-1.

---

## 6. Narrative / lore layer (optional depth)

Following Hades and Dark Souls: **a whole story for those who want it, invisible to those who just want to climb.**

- Lore is **carried by the living AI NPCs** — they hold histories and reveal them through conversation, remembering what *you* have uncovered. This is nearly free engineering (it rides the existing memory system); the cost is *writing*, and it scales with player interest.
- The Tower is the mystery box: what is it, who built it, what's at the top, who are the Rankers and factions.
- Never gates gameplay. A player can climb forever and never read a line of lore; an invested player finds a deep, personal, remembered story.

---

## 7. Design lenses (non-negotiable feel)

Two things the maker stated as **requirements**. Every feature must pass both:

- **Clippable — cool *and* funny.** Play must generate shareable moments. *Cool:* beating a brutal guardian, a Ranker's dramatic last-second save, a build popping off, screen-clearing spell combos. *Funny:* AI NPC roasts, chaotic co-op, absurd emergent situations. **Implication:** juice (screenshake, particles, hit-stop), spectacle spells, and *shareable AI reactions* are first-class, not polish afterthoughts.
- **Incredibly addictive — "hop on when bored."** Short sessions (a climb = ~5–15 min), instant pick-up-and-play, a tight loop with frequent rewards (loot every room, progress every climb), always something to chase (next floor, next rank, next unlock, next NPC lore beat), and roguelite meta-progression so *even a failed climb advances you.* **Implication:** friction to start a climb must be near-zero; the loop must pull "one more."

---

## 8. The core loop

**Landing/hub** (talk to living NPCs, set build/aura) → **Climb** (3–5 procedural rooms of top-down spell combat) → **Floor guardian** (hard, learnable) → **ascend or fall** → **back to the landing** (NPCs remember and react to the climb) → repeat, accreting loot, rank, and legend.

---

## 9. Combat model

- **View:** top-down twin-stick. Virtual joystick to move; **auto-aim** casting by default (Soul Knight / Archero touch model). **Dash** as core mobility and skill expression.
- **Casting:** **loadout + cooldowns.** 4 spell slots drawn from a growing pool. Build identity = which spells you slot + synergies. Touch-friendly, deep, the natural home for classes/ranks/loot. (Wizards of Legend's arcana. *Not* Magicka on-the-fly element combining — too input-heavy for touch.)
- **Class:** one class in the slice (a mage). More classes are post-proof content.

---

## 10. Progression & aura

Axes (all named by the maker):
- **Loot** — spell drops / boons mid-climb change your build.
- **Movement** — dash upgrades (blink, phase, speed) as an expressive axis.
- **Options** — build variety from the spell pool.
- **Ranks** — meta-progression = how high you've climbed (Tower ascension).
- **Aura** — semantic identity (§4.3): the world reads and narrates who you are.

Longevity comes from roguelite structure + accreting legend + the endless Tower, not hand-authored content volume.

---

## 11. The multiplayer ladder (the social dream, staged)

The endgame is an **MMO-*feel*** — the maker's words were "sort of," "somehow," "almost." That's load-bearing: the goal is the *feeling* of a living, populated Tower, **not a real MMO.**

**Real MMO** (persistent shared world: dedicated servers, matchmaking, accounts backend, world-state sync, anti-cheat, ongoing hosting cost) = solo-dev graveyard. **We do not build this.**

**MMO-feel** (Warframe / Destiny / Souls model: private instances + social landings + drop-in summoning, P2P/player-hosted, no persistent world) = ~90% of the feeling for ~10% of the cost. **This is the target.**

**The ladder — order is sacred, each rung ships:**
1. **Slice 0/1 — SP loop.** Prove climb + living hub are fun. *(weeks)*
2. **Local co-op (LAN).** Friends in the room. First netcode, lowest risk.
3. **Online drop-in "summoning" = Rankers descending.** The OP-player-helps-you fantasy; grinders' reward is heroism. Session-based, P2P-friendly, no persistent world. Fuses pillars 2 + 3.
4. **Public social + wave landings.** Visible players, horde-together — the MMO-feel summit. Most infrastructure, done last, on a proven base.

**The MMO-feel is the summit. We protect the dream by not falling off rung one reaching for rung four.**

**Design tension for the co-op era (not now):** the living hub is inherently *personal/private* (it remembers *you*); a public "see everyone" landing is *shared*. Reconciliation (Warframe-style): a **private chronicler landing** (your Raebai, your legend) + **separate public social/wave landings.**

## 12. Vertical slices (scope discipline — this is the point)

We are **not** building the whole game. We hunt the smallest slice that proves the concept is fun.

### Slice 0 — "Is it fun to move and cast?" *(days, not weeks)*
- One room, one class, joystick + dash + auto-aim casting, 2 enemy types.
- No run structure, no hub, no progression.
- Already carries the **clippable** lens: juice (hit-stop, screenshake, particles) so a single fight *feels* good.
- **If this isn't fun, nothing else matters — prove it first, cheaply.**
- **Acceptance:** moving, dashing, and casting *feels good* in the maker's hands. Subjective, and correct.

### Slice 1 — "The Climb." *(the real proof)*
- A short climb: 3–4 procedural rooms → **one floor guardian** (the first hard boss).
- 2–3 enemy types; 1–2 boons per climb that meaningfully change the build.
- Return to a **one-landing hub with Raebai**, who *remembers the climb* (which guardian, ascend/fall, playstyle) and reacts — reusing the four-layer memory + Ollama + consolidation.
- A **seed of aura:** Raebai reads a simple reputation signal ("reckless" vs "careful") and reflects it.
- **The entire unique game in miniature** — climb, hard guardian, run structure, and the living-hub moat, all minimal, all present.
- **Acceptance:** a full loop plays end-to-end; the guardian is genuinely hard; Raebai's reaction references the actual climb and lands emotionally.

Everything else — co-op, more classes/floors/guardians, deep aura, lore depth, voice, generative content — is built **only after** Slice 1 is proven fun.

## 14. Architecture principles

- **Authority-clean from day one.** Build combat/entities so a host/authority split can be added later without a rewrite — cheap insurance so drop-in co-op (rungs 2–3) slots in cleanly. We are *not* writing netcode in the slice, and *not* painting into a single-player-only corner.
- **LLM only for deliberate moments** (unchanged from `architecture.md`): never in combat, pathfinding, or per-frame logic. The hub reaction is a deliberate between-climbs moment — the correct and only place the LLM runs.
- **Placeholder-first art** (proven v0.0–v0.5 loop): shapes / asset-pack pixel art to prove feel; real art later.
- **Godot 4 + GDScript**, mobile export target (Android first).

- The 3D work on `v1.0-3d` is preserved and parked; nothing is deleted.
- Proposed branch name: `v2.0-tower`.

## 16. Explicitly out of scope (YAGNI)

Named so they don't creep into the slice:
- A real persistent-world MMO (§11).
- Online netcode of any kind (rungs 2–4 come after Slice 1).
- Voiced NPCs (local TTS) — magic, but polish-tier, later.
- Generative rooms / quests / events — used *surgically* later (dialogue/event flavor), never a crutch, never in the slice.
- Multiple classes, deep skill trees, extensive floor/guardian roster, meta-progression economy — all post-Slice-1.
- Deep lore content — the *system* rides the memory layer for near-free, but writing the Tower's story is a post-proof effort.
- The public/shared landing reconciliation (§11) — co-op era.

## 20. Session addendum (2026-07-09) — the stick-figure fighting-feel direction (maker, mid-build, AFK autonomy granted)

Captured live as the maker refined the vision and then said *"just make Stick Fight the game basically, we can start there."* This SHARPENS the north star; it does not discard §§1–18. See memory [[project_v2_combat_usp_stickfigure]].

**The sharpened pitch:** *"Stick Fight, with magic + animations + classes + gear + a large layered map + local co-op."* Fighting FEEL is the USP; simple to play, spectacular to watch.

- **Stick-figure aesthetic (art direction, locked as the working direction).** Characters are stick figures; the world is built around that (Stick Fight). Cheap to animate = the biggest solo-dev art win yet. **All spectacle lives in the EFFECTS/animation layer:** punch/kick/cast animation, spell VFX, and **telegraph circles that bloom before a GIANT blast** (Cuphead/boss language, pillar 4). Build a swappable procedural rig now → real animated stick sprites drop in later.
- **Fighting feel = the USP (elevates §7 + §19 to primary).** Punch, kick, AND cast — a stick-figure spell-BRAWLER. **Melee is now in scope** alongside spell-loadout (a genuine addition to §9's spell-only combat).
- **Classes as stick figures.** Mage / rogue / summoner "and all the classes," each distinct via effects/abilities; "high-level" = spectacular powered-up forms. (Roster is post-core content; the RIG must support it from day one.)
- **Equipment = visual + functional.** Hat / robe / sandals / swords "all the type" — gear layers onto the stick rig (cosmetic) AND confers abilities (Terraria gear-drives-look-and-power). The rig has **equipment slots** (head/body/feet/weapon) from the start.
- **Map structure resolved (partially).** Terraria-feel is the **"story mode"** flavor over a **large, layered, open-world-exploratory map with a hub** — Terraria-style layers: some layers are **multiplayer zones**, others are **mob-spawning zones with escalating difficulty**. This RECONCILES the §12 tension: not strict ephemeral roguelite runs — a layered explorable world with a hub. (Roguelite-run structure can live inside layers; not settled — revisit with maker.)
- **Local co-op** is in the core "that's all we're building" set (still MP-ladder rung 2 per §11 — netcode after the SP core feels good).
- **Anime-inspired powers** (Naruto / Dragon Ball energy attacks) = post-launch CONTENT drops, not core scope.

**Start-here scope (what "just make Stick Fight the game" means for the build):** the combat core — stick-figure rig + animation states + melee (punch/kick) + cast + dash + effects (telegraph→giant-blast, juice) + SFX — built on the existing `scenes/combat/` toy. Roadmap: `docs/v2.0-stickfight-core-plan.md`.

**Open forks for the maker (do not silently resolve):** (1) large-layered-map vs roguelite-runs-inside-layers; (2) the AI-memory NPC hub — the shipped v0.0–v0.5 "moat" — was absent from the fighting-USP framing; it is PRESERVED, not cut, pending the maker's explicit re-scope.

## 21. Session addendum (2026-07-10) — Slice 2 shipped: the loop closes, the moat reconnects

Built autonomously on maker directive "work on all four threads." Slice 2 turns the standalone combat toy into a game loop and folds the shipped v0.0–v0.5 AI-memory NPCs back in. See `docs/v2.0-slice2-checklist.md`.

**Fork resolutions (from §20's open forks):**
- **Fork 1 (map structure) — resolved TOWARD roguelite floor-rooms now** (strategy-subagent rec: rooms now, big explorable layered map after Slice 1/2 proves combat fun). A run = 5 floors; clear-to-exit climbing; per-floor theme bands (surface → underground → sky) give the Terraria-layer flavour cheaply. The large persistent world is deferred, not cancelled — it grows out of the floor bands once combat is proven fun.
- **Fork 2 (AI-memory hub) — resolved KEEP + reconnect NOW.** The shipped Raebai/Mirelle memory hub (`Main.tscn`) is now the game's home base: F5 boots into the hub, a portal enters the tower, and on return the NPCs **remember the run** (floor reached, died vs cleared, element used) via the existing four-layer memory plumbing — no new memory system, no save-schema bump. "Combat = gameplay USP, AI hub = marketing USP" is now literally wired: your deaths get remembered and referenced.

**What shipped (7 commits, headless-verified, unplaytested):**
- `GameState` autoload — the HUB↔RUN spine + run-outcome record + run-fact injection into NPC memory + floor math + `selected_class`.
- Roguelite floor loop in `Arena.gd` (RUN mode) with the legacy endless SANDBOX mode preserved for F6 direct-combat playtesting.
- Hub↔run wiring: tower-entrance portal, run ingest into NPC memory, one-shot callback run-hint. Boot scene → `Main.tscn`.
- **Second class: ROGUE** (dash-strike assassin) + live class switch (Tab). Mage unchanged. Class substrate (`CLASS_CONFIG`) is ready for summoner/etc.
- **Two telegraphed enemies:** ranged CASTER (dodge the bolt) + lane CHARGER (sidestep the charge), reusing the Telegraph primitive (new additive LINE mode).
- Live tuning surface (`Tuning` autoload + `tuning.tres`) for the hot hero feel knobs.

**Still open / deferred (unchanged intent):** floor-5 guardian is a dense wave, not yet a bespoke multi-phase boss (§20 pillar 4); hub class-select UI (Tab is the debug stand-in); HP does not carry hub↔run; the large layered explorable map (Fork 1's "later" half); PvP (§19); real animated sprites over the procedural rig. Elements remain cosmetic; rank grants no stats.
