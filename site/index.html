The climb is the game

Floors are authored, not shuffled — ten of them, each its own biome, each with something in it that has been waiting.

Nine classes, no recolours

Every class gets a signature spell and a movement verb nobody else has. If two of them play the same, one of them is wrong.

Made for a phone, honestly

One screen per floor, two players, no menus between you and the fight. It runs on the device you already have.

Get told when it's out

Two emails, ever: one when there's something playable, one when it launches. No newsletter, no drip sequence, unsubscribe in a click.

Stickspire is made by one person. The list is how you hear about it before an algorithm decides whether to show you.
